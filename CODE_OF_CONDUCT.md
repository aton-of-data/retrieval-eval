# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to get something right.

## Specifics

Expected: welcoming and patient language, honest technical disagreement, gracefully accepting
correction, and focusing on what is best for people using the project.

Not acceptable: personal attacks, harassment, demeaning or discriminatory comments, publishing
others' private information, or sustained disruption.

Technical criticism is not a conduct problem. This project's whole method is checking claims
and reporting what turns out to be false, including its own maintainers' claims. Saying "this
measurement is wrong and here is why" is the point. Saying it about a person is not.

## Scope and enforcement

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is representing the
project.

Report concerns to the maintainers via a private GitHub security advisory or direct contact.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, delete or reject contributions and
ban accounts for behaviour they judge inappropriate; they are expected to explain moderation
decisions when asked.
